Privacy Policy

My Daily Verse AI Publisher (Pinterest API application)
Effective date: 13 September 2026

Who we are

My Daily Verse AI is a devotional content brand publishing daily Bible verse content on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest.

Contact: [email protected]

What this app is

"My Daily Verse AI Publisher" is a private application that connects to the Pinterest API solely to publish content to the brand's own Pinterest business account (@mydailyverseai). The app is used only by the account owner. It is not offered to the public and has no end users.

What data the app accesses

Through Pinterest's official OAuth 2.0 authorisation flow, the app can access the connected account's boards and pins for the purpose of publishing content to that account. The app accesses no other Pinterest user data.

What data the app stores

The app stores:

• The OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Pinterest, kept securely on the owner's self-hosted, access-controlled server for the purpose of maintaining the connection.
• The content being published (images, titles, descriptions), created and owned by the brand.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of Pinterest users or any third party. API data is not cached beyond what is required for the immediate publishing action.

Analytics and tracking

The app performs no analytics, tracking, or profiling of any user.

Third-party sharing

No data accessed through the Pinterest API is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Data deletion

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time via Pinterest account settings. Upon revocation, stored OAuth tokens are deleted. Requests regarding this policy can be sent to the contact email above.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

This policy is published to satisfy Pinterest's developer requirements for API access.
